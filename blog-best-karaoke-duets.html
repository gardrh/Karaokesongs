The 20 Best Karaoke Duets for Any Occasion

4 min read

There's something magical about a great karaoke duet. The shared responsibility, the harmonies (attempted or otherwise), the eye contact at the good bits — it transforms a solo performance into a shared moment. Whether you're heading up with a romantic partner, your best friend, or a sibling who's had just enough drinks to agree, the right duet can be the highlight of the whole night.

Here are 20 of the best karaoke duets ever written, organised by occasion so you can find the right one for your night.

Perfect for couples, or for anyone who wants to create a slightly awkward but undeniably moving moment with their best friend.

Tips for a Great Karaoke Duet

Agree on the song before you go up. The worst duets happen when one person is more enthusiastic than the other. Make sure you're both genuinely into it.

Divide the verses clearly. Know who's singing what before you start. Confusion about who takes the lead on a verse is death to a duet.

Make eye contact. It feels weird, but it transforms the performance. The crowd responds to connection between the two singers — give it to them.

Harmonise on the chorus if you can. Even rough harmonies on a big chorus feel incredible. Don't be afraid to try.
