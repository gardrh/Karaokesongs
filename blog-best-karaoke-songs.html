50 Best Karaoke Songs of All Time

5 min read

There are thousands of songs in the average karaoke catalogue. Most of them are forgettable. A few of them are legendary. These are the ones that get people out of their seats, singing along even when it's not their turn, and ordering another round just because the energy is so good.

We've pulled together the 50 best karaoke songs of all time — songs that work across genres, across generations, and across every type of crowd from birthday parties to corporate nights out. Whether you're a karaoke veteran or stepping up to the mic for the first time, this list has something for you.

How to Use This List

Don't just pick the most famous song on the list — pick the one that fits you and the mood. A huge banger sung nervously lands worse than a smaller song sung with total confidence. Know your voice, feel the room, and commit completely.
