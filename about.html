About SingPin!

What is this?

SingPin! is the best karaoke song picker in the world — and we're not being modest.

In a world overflowing with music, deciding what to sing next at karaoke can be genuinely hard. SingPin! exists to save you from singing the same song you always do. Our mission: rescue the world from repetitive karaoke choices, one song at a time.

How does it work?

Select the type of song you're feeling — or go with All — then hit the big SING! button as many times as you want. Easy like Sunday morning.

You can also try MoodMatcher — answer 5 quick questions and we'll find your perfect match.

How are songs categorized?

Rather than traditional genres, SingPin! uses mood-based types:

😄 Happy! — feel-good crowd pleasers
💙 Ballad! — emotional, slow burners
🔥 Banger! — high-energy crowd hypers
🤘 Rock! — guitar-driven anthems
👫 Duet! — songs for two
✨ Soulful! — show off your voice
