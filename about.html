About Singpin!

What is this website?

SingPin! is the best karaoke song picker in the world!

In a world of music, it can often be difficult to decide which karaoke song you are going to sing next. To help you, inspire you and encourage you to try find and try out new karaoke songs, Singpin! has been created. Our mission is to save the world from repetitive karaoke song choices, one song at the time.

How does it work?

To find your song, simply select the type of song you want to sing, or select all types, then press the button "SING!" as many times as you want. Easy like Sunday morning!

How are the songs categorized?

The karaoke song database used on this page consists of popular songs that can be sung at karaoke. Rather than categorize them in genres, we have decided to use types of songs: "Happy!", "Ballad!", "Banger!", "Rock!", "Duet!" and "Soulful!"

The songs are categorized into different types through careful consideration of the type of mood they convey. If you want to sing a happy song, the type "Happy!" is likely to provide you with a type of song you are looking after.
